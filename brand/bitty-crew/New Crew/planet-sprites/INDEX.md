# Planet population sprites v1 — 2026-08-23

11 characters. 32×32. Side view. 4-frame walk at 170 ms (loop). Shared 31-color palette including transparent. Hard alpha.

Walks are pixel-shifts of legs/hem/near arm plus a 1px bob. Not redrawn stride art. Source is AI JPEG “pixel-looking” sheets, 8×8 snapped. Readable at planet scale. Does not survive a zoom-in.

Only `man_walk_v1.pxo` was saved from Pixelorama. The rest match that format (128×32 sheet, GIF, 4× preview).
